# Everfresh chamber: start here

Ara's brief for the next Everfresh grow chamber. It pins down what the chamber has to do; the cabinet itself is yours.

**In this folder**
- `EVERFRESH-BRIEF.md`: the full spec your AI reads.
- `Everfresh-builder-brief.pdf`: the two-page version for you.
- `everfresh-zones.png`: what goes where and why (zones, not a layout).
- `v1-front.jpg`, `v1-lid.jpg`: today's chamber.

**Getting your AI going**
1. Put these files in the folder your AI assistant works in.
2. Open Rhino with a new document and start a fresh chat.
3. Paste this:

```
Read EVERFRESH-BRIEF.md in this folder and look at everfresh-zones.png and the two v1 photos. Before you model anything, reply with a short list: the job as you understand it, what's fixed and what's open, and your questions for me. After I answer, start the functional skeleton in my open Rhino document, as the brief describes.
```

4. Answer its questions. It builds the skeleton first (parts, clearances, air and water paths) and checks in before drawing any cabinet.

**Sending things to Ara**
Screenshots of the model (perspective, front, side section), plus questions as a numbered list, one per line, each with your suggested answer. He replies from his phone.
